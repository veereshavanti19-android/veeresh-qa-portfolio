QA Approach

Understand. Analyze. Test. Break it. Verify it.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Understand

Study requirements, user journeys, business rules and expected behaviour before designing test coverage.

02 / ANALYZE

Analyze

Identify risks, dependencies, boundary conditions and areas where failures could have the highest impact.

03 / TEST

Test

Validate functional flows, APIs, mobile behaviour, integrations, data and business logic.

04 / BREAK

Break

Challenge the happy path with invalid input, unexpected sequences, authorization and edge cases.

05 / VERIFY

Verify

Validate fixes, perform regression testing and confirm the system behaves correctly after changes.
