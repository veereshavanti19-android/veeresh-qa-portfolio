02

Testing Approach

A defect is only the beginning. The investigation matters.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Understand

Understand the product workflow, requirements, user roles and business expectations before testing.

02 / ANALYZE

Analyze

Identify risk areas, dependencies, validations and scenarios where the system is most likely to fail.

03 / TEST

Test

Execute positive, negative, boundary, regression, API, mobile and exploratory scenarios.

04 / BREAK

Break

Challenge assumptions with invalid inputs, unauthorized actions, unexpected flows and edge cases.

05 / VERIFY

Verify

Retest fixes, validate impacted areas and confirm the final behaviour meets the expected outcome.
